TeaTime · Phase 0 · Design directions

Three looks for Today

The same Today screen drawn three ways, each in light and dark. Pick the one TeaTime should become, or tell me what to mix from each.

Picked on 20 November 2026: A · Warm journal. B and C stay here as the record.

Everything on the screens is an illustrative sample: #017 Moroccan mint, its story and its quantities are placeholders, not sourced content yet. The screens respond: change the cups, tap a rating, pause the timer, or peek at tomorrow's tea.

All three keep SPEC §3.8: every tap target is 44 px or more, control borders reach 3:1 against their surface, button text is 16 px (the tab bar's captions aside), and the layout keeps clear of the safe areas.

Direction A

Warm journal

Cream paper and tea-brown ink, with Alegreya for the names and stories. It reads like the notebook the codes go into: a margin rule, a ribbon, and the story written on the lines.

Alegreya for names and stories, Alegreya Sans for the rest.

Direction B

Calm minimal

Near-white and near-black, Geist throughout, and one leaf green kept for what is happening now: the running timer and Made it. Hairlines instead of boxes, and room to breathe.

Geist for text, Geist Mono for numbers and timers.

Direction C

Ink & washi

Washi paper, sumi-black ink and a red seal for the blend's number, after a Japanese tea house. Square edges, a brushed ensō for the running timer, and the seal used nowhere else.

Shippori Mincho for names and stories, Zen Kaku Gothic for the rest.
